Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the "LocalHub" application (hereinafter referred to as "the App") developed by independent developer Baron Lee. We value your privacy, and this policy aims to explain how we collect, use, and protect your information.

English Version

1. Core Commitment: Local First

LocalHub shares files and media between your devices. Local file transfers do not use our cloud file storage. Optional usage statistics, crash reports, and Google Play billing use separate online services as described below.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

To provide our services, we may process the following information:

  • Device and Network Information: LocalHub uses your local IP address to establish its sharing server; connected devices can see that address. Local IP addresses are not included in our custom analytics event parameters. Optional online services involve network connections whose providers may process IP addresses. Google Analytics can use an IP address to derive approximate location before discarding it; the App does not collect precise GPS location for analytics. Google Analytics regional data collection
  • App Usage and Performance Data (Optional): With your explicit consent, release builds use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for usage statistics and crash diagnostics. Data can include installation and session identifiers, device and system information, app versions, feature events, transfer byte counts, error details and diagnostic logs. These identifiers support event and crash deduplication and can link reports from the same installation. We do not send shared file contents as analytics event parameters. You can disable future collection in App Settings; disabling collection does not itself delete reports already received by the provider.
  • Install Attribution (Optional): With the same analytics consent, the App reads Google Play install attribution once and sends only normalized campaign fields (source, medium, campaign, and content) to Firebase Analytics. The raw referrer and advertising click identifiers are not stored or uploaded by the App.
  • Transaction Information: Premium is currently a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. The App receives purchase records, product IDs, purchase tokens and entitlement status, and acknowledges purchases through Google Play Billing. We do not receive bank card details.

3. GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) Notice

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and UK:

  • Legal Basis: We process your personal data based on your consent (for analytics/crash reporting) and for the performance of a contract (providing the file sharing service).
  • Your Rights: You have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. You may withdraw your consent for analytics at any time in the app settings.
  • Data Retention: Usage statistics follow the retention settings of our Google Analytics property and applicable service rules. You can contact us to ask about the current setting or request deletion. Firebase states that Crashlytics normally retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. Local connection state is cleared when the server stops; received files remain on your device until deleted. Firebase data and retention information
  • International Transfers: Firebase services may process data in the United States under standard contractual clauses or other lawful mechanisms.

4. CCPA/CPRA (California Privacy Rights) Notice

For California residents:

  • We Do Not Sell Your Personal Information. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • We Do Not Share Your Personal Information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Your Rights: You have the right to request access to and deletion of your personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights.

5. Permissions Usage

The App requests permissions (Camera, Storage, Notifications, Location/Nearby Devices) only when necessary for core functionality. Location permission is solely used for local hotspot/Wi-Fi management and not for tracking your geographical location.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase: Used for crash analysis and data statistics (only with consent). View Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution, billing settlement, and consent-gated install attribution. View Privacy Policy

中文版 (Chinese Version)

1. 核心承诺：本地优先

LocalHub 用于在你的设备之间共享文件和媒体。局域网文件传输不使用我们的云端文件存储。可选使用统计、崩溃报告和 Google Play 计费会使用下述独立在线服务。

2. 信息收集与使用

  • 设备与网络信息： LocalHub 使用局域网 IP 建立共享服务器，已连接设备可以看到该地址。我们的自定义分析事件参数不包含局域网 IP。可选在线服务涉及网络连接，其提供方可能处理 IP 地址；Google Analytics 可先据此推导大致位置，再丢弃 IP。应用不为分析采集精确 GPS 位置。Google Analytics 区域数据说明
  • 应用分析与崩溃报告（可选）： 经你明确同意后，正式版使用 Firebase Analytics 与 Crashlytics 处理使用统计和稳定性诊断。数据可能包含安装及会话标识、设备与系统信息、应用版本、功能事件、传输字节数、错误详情和诊断日志；标识用于事件及崩溃去重，能够关联同一次安装的报告。共享文件内容不作为分析事件参数发送。你可以在应用设置中关闭后续收集；关闭收集本身不会删除提供方已经接收的报告。
  • 安装来源归因（可选）： 在同一分析统计授权下，应用仅会读取一次 Google Play 安装来源，并向 Firebase Analytics 发送规范化后的渠道字段（来源、媒介、活动和素材）。应用不会保存或上传原始 referrer 与广告点击标识符。
  • 交易信息： 当前 Premium 为 Google Play 一次性买断。应用接收购买记录、商品 ID、purchase token 和权益状态，并通过 Google Play Billing 确认购买，不接触银行卡信息。

3. 用户权利与数据保护

  • 选择权： 您可以完全控制是否开启分析统计功能。
  • 数据保留： 使用统计按 Google Analytics 项目的保留设置及服务规则处理；你可以联系我们查询当前设置或申请删除。Firebase 说明 Crashlytics 通常保留崩溃堆栈及关联标识 90 天后，开始从在线及备份系统移除。本地连接状态在服务停止时清空，已接收的文件仍保留在设备上，直到删除。Firebase 数据与保留说明
  • 未成年人保护： 本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。

4. 权限使用

我们仅在必要时请求权限（如相机、存储、通知、位置/附近设备）。位置权限仅用于局域网/热点功能，不用于地理位置追踪。
